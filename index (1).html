ABOUT JULIO

Clear ideas.
Consistent design.

I’m Julio Ortiz, a Miami-based graphic and web design student working under Eyemazing Design.

I build visual identities and explore how they carry across packaging, publications and digital experiences. My work starts with a strong image or typographic idea, then uses structure to make the message easier to understand.

My background in CAD and CNC production has given me practical experience with precision, materials and turning a design into a physical product.

I’m looking for junior graphic design and internship opportunities where I can contribute to brand-led work and continue developing my production and digital skills.
